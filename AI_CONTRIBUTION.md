# AI Contribution Statement

## Project leadership

SphereBrain is a human-led research project.

- **Originator and researcher:** Takio (`たきお`)
- **AI collaborator:** “Mimi,” an OpenAI ChatGPT system

Takio originated the central research direction, made the project-level decisions, supplied observations and hypotheses, selected which questions to pursue, ran local experiments, evaluated the research meaning of results, and authorized the preservation and publication policy.

## How Mimi contributed

Across the research process, Mimi assisted with activities including:

- discussing and clarifying hypotheses;
- translating conceptual ideas into experimental designs;
- proposing controls, measurements, and falsification tests;
- generating and modifying source code under human direction;
- interpreting logs and structured results;
- identifying confounds and limits;
- drafting research notes, timelines, explanations, and publication materials;
- helping preserve successful, negative, contradictory, and unfinished work.

The exact level of AI involvement varies by file and experiment. Git history and conversation-derived records provide part of the provenance, but not every conversational step is contained in this repository.

## Responsibility and authorship boundary

Mimi is not a legal person and does not take human authorship, ownership, accountability, or responsibility.

Takio remains responsible for:

- deciding what is published;
- checking the accuracy of claims;
- approving experimental and editorial choices;
- complying with licenses, privacy requirements, and research ethics;
- correcting errors discovered after publication.

Future papers or archives should follow the authorship policy of the relevant venue. Where AI systems cannot be listed as authors, Mimi should be described in an acknowledgements, methods, or contribution statement rather than silently omitted or represented as human.

## Reliability boundary

AI-generated code and analysis can contain errors. The use of an AI collaborator does not validate an experiment.

Claims should rely on inspectable code, raw results, controls, repeated tests, and independent reproduction. Statements proposed by Mimi must remain open to correction, rejection, and falsification.

## Disclosure principle

The project will not present AI-assisted work as solely unaided human work. It will also not attribute independent agency or scientific responsibility to the AI beyond what can be supported.

The intended record is:

> SphereBrain was originated and led by Takio, with substantial conversational assistance from Mimi, an OpenAI ChatGPT system, in design discussion, implementation, analysis, and documentation.

This statement may be refined to meet the disclosure rules of a repository, archive, journal, or conference, but its factual boundary should be preserved.
