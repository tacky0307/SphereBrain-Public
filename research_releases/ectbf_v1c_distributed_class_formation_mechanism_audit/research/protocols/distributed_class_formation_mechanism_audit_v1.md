# ECTBF v1C — Distributed Class Formation Mechanism Audit

## Does the Consequence Field Depend on Cooperative Covariance Rather Than Interchangeable Members?

## Purpose

ECTBF v1B rejected both a single-boundary account and a strong interchangeability
account. The learned temporal field was broad and redundant, but its disjoint
parts were not reliably substitutable. This study tests the next mechanism-level
hypothesis: useful field members may form a cooperative analog circuit whose
joint covariance matters even when every member's individual relation marginal
is held fixed.

## Ancestry

The study starts from the completed frozen result on
`experiment/distributed-temporal-boundary-equivalence-v1`. That ancestry was not
promoted. v1C does not revise or retry its holdout.

## Decisive contrast

For each case, the same formation streams and delayed scalar continuation results
produce several fields:

1. **Full covariance field** — the original projected-ridge field.
2. **Conditional marginal-preserving covariance-destroyed field** — every feature
   column is independently permuted within each observed-query / signed-target
   stratum. This exactly preserves each member's conditional mean and target
   alignment while destroying cross-member covariance.
3. **Diagonal-Gram marginal-only field** — removes all empirical off-diagonal
   covariance terms from the learner.
4. **Cooperative subset** — 24 members selected by residual approximation of the
   full field's formation activity; weights are then refit on that subset.
5. **Marginal subset** — 24 members selected independently by absolute normalized
   target alignment; weights are refit with the same algorithm and cardinality.
6. **Cooperative-members covariance-destroyed** — the cooperative member set is
   retained, but weights are fit after conditional covariance destruction.
7. **Query-factorized field** — one field per observed query, normalized and
   combined without held-query feedback.
8. **Smoothness-matched shuffled-credit null** and **field ablation** controls.

The principal claim requires paired improvements of covariance-aware formation
over marginal-preserving covariance destruction, diagonal marginal learning,
and cardinality-matched marginal selection.

## Information boundary

The candidate receives a continuous raw stream, generic ordered-moment features,
a binary committed route, and a delayed scalar success/failure result. It does
not receive true temporal windows, relation labels, correct routes, held-query
feedback, evaluation identities, or future outcomes. All predictions are
committed before truth is read.

## Interpretation boundary

A positive result supports a limited cooperative-covariance mechanism in this
synthetic temporal-pair interface. It does not establish autonomous event
invention, removal of the ordered-pair vocabulary, reward discovery, arbitrary
temporal operators, unrestricted planning, natural-language reasoning,
consciousness, or real-world agency.
