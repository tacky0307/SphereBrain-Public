## Phase I scope

The Phase I publication records the Natural Law Study through ID1R, including both positive and negative results.

The main observations include:

- experience-dependent Landscape formation
- non-additive Whole-State interaction under plastic dynamics
- repeated maturation and saturation
- metaplastic Consolidation and differentiated retention lifetimes
- temporal-spacing dependence
- intervening-experience / primitive history-context dependence
- limited experience-order imprinting

The publication also preserves important negative results, including failed or unsupported predictive structure, native temporal directionality, universal order sensitivity, and sharp repetition-count lifetime thresholds.

See:

- `docs/NATURAL_LAW_STUDY_PHASE_I.md`
- `docs/NATURAL_LAW_STUDY_PHASE_I_JA.md`
- `LIMITATIONS.md`
- `EXPERIMENT_AUDIT.md`
- `REPRODUCE.md`

## Claim boundaries

Phase I does not establish:

- language understanding
- semantic concepts inside the Core
- free recall
- reliable prediction
- general temporal directionality
- causal reasoning
- episodic memory
- human-like cognition
- general intelligence
- biological equivalence to the brain

The results remain limited to the tested artificial worlds, topologies, parameter ranges, and measurement procedures.

## Experience-grounded prediction checkpoint — 2026-09-18

Public checkpoint added at:

research_releases/2026-09-18_experience_prediction_checkpoint/

It covers Progress-Gated Resumption / Two Keys, Flip Learning Loop v1–v2, Route Readout v1, reusable route-selection v2, and ambiguity-preserving route families v3.

**Status:** research checkpoint only. No mechanism in this line is promoted into the canonical Core by this commit.

The strongest attribution limitation is that ordinary experience storage using the same higher-level rule matched the route-based system in the latest controls.
